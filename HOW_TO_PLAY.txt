========================================================================
             ADVENTURE HILL RESCUE - HOW TO PLAY & SHARE
========================================================================

FOR YOUR FRIEND:
1. Double-click 'Play_Game.bat' (or simply double-click 'index.html').
2. That's it! The game will launch in your default web browser (Chrome,
   Edge, Firefox, Safari).

CONTROLS:
- RUN: A / D  or  Left / Right Arrow keys
- CLIMB LADDERS: W / S  or  Up / Down Arrow keys
- JUMP: Spacebar (Daddy toots when he jumps over barrels! ??)
- RETURN TO HOME: Click 'Home' button or press 'H' key
- PAUSE: P key

Enjoy the adventure!
