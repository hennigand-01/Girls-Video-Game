# 🌟 Adventure Hill Rescue! 🌟

A fun, vibrant Donkey Kong-style arcade video game made for Ilianna and Ava! Jump over rolling barrels, climb ladders up zig-zag mountain paths, grab sparkling power-ups, and race to the mountain summit to rescue the cute lost pet!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Just Double-Click to Play!
Simply double-click [`index.html`](./index.html) to launch the game instantly in any browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox). No installation or downloads required!

## 🕹️ Game Controls

### ⌨️ Keyboard
| Action | Primary Key | Alternate Key |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Run Left** | `A` | `Left Arrow (◀)` |
| **Run Right** | `D` | `Right Arrow (▶)` |
| **Climb Up Ladder** | `W` | `Up Arrow (▲)` |
| **Climb Down Ladder** | `S` | `Down Arrow (▼)` |
| **Jump Over Barrels** | `Spacebar` | Touch `⬆️ JUMP` Button |
| **Pause Game** | `P` | — |

### 📱 Touch / Tablet Controls
- **D-Pad Buttons** on the bottom left for moving and climbing.
- **Big Blue JUMP Button** on the bottom right for jumping over rolling obstacles.

## 🏃 Fluid Movement & Pixar-Level Character Detail

- **Dynamic Squash & Stretch**: Characters compress and spring when jumping and landing on logs.
- **Slope Tilt**: Characters lean naturally into sloped sticks as they run uphill or downhill.
- **Responsive Platforming**: Includes variable jump cut, coyote time grace periods, jump input buffering, and skidding dust clouds.
- **Secondary Animation**: Wind-blown hair, fluttering butterfly hair clips, swinging crystal earrings, Daddy's bouncy belly spring physics, and fairy star trails.

## 🔨 Power-Ups & Collectibles

- **🔨 The Hammer (Classic Donkey Kong Style!)**: Pick up the mallet to enter **Hammer Time** for 12 seconds! Run into rolling barrels to shatter them into flying wooden splinters, sawdust, and chunks (`+300` points; or `+350` Daddy Toot Smash!).
- **🪄 Magic Bubble Wand**: Gives you a rainbow Bubble Shield! Bumping into barrels pops them harmlessly into bubbles (`+200` points!).
- **👟 Spring Boots**: Super-charges your jump height so you can vault high over obstacles!
- **💎 Sparkling Forest Jewels**: Collect glistening rubies and sapphires for bonus points!
- **❤️ Extra Hearts**: Restores a lost heart to keep you in the adventure.
- **🌟 Barrel Jump Bonus**: Time your jump directly over rolling barrels for bonus points, star bursts (or funny toot clouds if playing as Daddy)!

Have fun climbing the mountain and setting high scores! 🌟
